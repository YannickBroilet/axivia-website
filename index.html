The problems behind the brief

Sometimes the real question is hiding behind the question.

“We have the technology. We don't know where to take it.”

Clarify the strongest application, user need and market opportunity before investing further.

“We have too many opportunities. Which ones deserve investment?”

Create a decision framework and prioritise opportunities against evidence and strategic fit.

“Our product is good. The market isn't responding.”

Challenge the value proposition, positioning, adoption barriers and market assumptions.

“R&D, Product and Sales don't agree on where to go.”

Create shared clarity around the customer, the opportunity, the trade-offs and the next decision.
